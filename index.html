The ugly truth

Standard corporate event formats are not designed to build connection.

Attend a panel. Network over dinner. Exchange cards. Leave without a meaningful conversation. Sounds familiar?

Attendance is not participation

A panel is watched, a dinner is endured. People leave having listened, but having rarely spoken.

The room is left to chance

Who is in the room matters more than what is on the stage. Most events put far more thought into stage design, full-day agendas and long presentations.

The pitch arrives before the relationship

When a gathering feels like a sales funnel, guests guard their attention, and the brand spends more to build that connection.

The vibe shift

Across the events landscape, size is losing its appeal. Planners are trading spectacle for substance, and building experiences that prioritize authentic human connection.

Skift Meetings, Mega Trends 2026

The argument isn't that standard formats are dead. It's that the event playbook is expanding, and the game has shifted from broadcasting information to designing for participation and connection.

“We exist because standard formats stopped creating meaningful connections.”

The vision is simple

Change existing, predictable formats to create niche experiences and new formats that foster authentic human connection.

“One of the reasons I started this was that I got tired of seeing large-scale events where no one actually connected. I challenged myself not to take the cookie-cutter approach to B2B events.”

Let's be clear

We are not an event agency.

  • We design the conversation format.
  • We shape an environment where important conversations happen naturally.
  • The host is present as a thoughtful convener, never the loudest voice in the room.
